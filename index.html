About

I am a postdoctoral researcher working on problems in cryptography and distributed computing. I completed my PhD in computer science at Aarhus University, supervised by Jesper Buus Nielsen. During my PhD, I visited Christian Cachin at the University of Bern, interned at Galois Inc., and worked at Concordium Research on consensus and cryptographic protocols. Since then I have been hosted by Julian Loss at the CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security and Ruhr University Bochum.
